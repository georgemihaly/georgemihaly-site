About

I'm an Emmy Award-winning producer who has spent my career where creative ambition meets hard constraints. I've crewed and led production teams across the globe for Fortune 500 companies, sports organizations, and media brands.

At Meta, I developed content and creative strategy for internal communications reaching 70,000 employees, trained teams in virtual production, and produced virtual and in-person events.

For CBS's Super Bowl LV, I led technical direction for remote production of the opening film, which won the 2021 Sports Emmy for Outstanding Opening/Tease.

I'm based in Portland, Oregon, and I'm outside whenever I can be, usually with my wife, our kids, and our dog.
